### About me

- 🔬 Ph.D. candidate in **Condensed Matter Physics** at the Universidade Federal do Ceará (UFC), where I also earned my bachelor's and master's degrees in Physics.
- 🌊 I simulate **wave packet dynamics in graphene**, in C, C++ and Python.
- 🤖 Since September 2025 I've been at the Fundação Cearense de Pesquisa e Cultura (FCPC), on the **Kunumi Lab UFC** project, customizing open-source large language models for process automation.
- ⚡ Finishing a technical degree in **Electrotechnics** at IFCE: microcontrollers, embedded firmware, instrumentation, signal conditioning and industrial control.
- 🌎 Based in Brazil · Portuguese and English
